About

Jennifer Navis

I’m Jennifer — a writer, entrepreneur, chronic idea-starter, and someone who has never been very good at having just one thing going on.

I spend part of my life building businesses, solving practical problems, making decisions and starting things from scratch. Writing is the quieter side of me — the place where I question, observe, invent, and make sense of what does not fit neatly anywhere else.

I love poetry for its precision, fiction for its freedom, and this website because not every thought needs to become a book before it deserves a place to exist.
