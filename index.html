Journal

Come sit with me for a while.

This is the less polished part of the site — the place where I write before everything has a label.

Sometimes it will be a poem. Sometimes a story. Sometimes just a thought I needed to put somewhere.

I write about love, memory, ambition, disappointment, people, ordinary moments, strange thoughts, and the things we often understand only after they are over.

Some pieces may grow into books. Some may remain exactly as they are.

You’re welcome to read, react, disagree, remember something of your own, or simply leave quietly.

Books

The Things I Don’t Say Aloud

My first poetry collection began with a simple truth: there are so many things we feel deeply and never say.

The words we swallow.

The conversations we replay after they are over.

The people we miss without telling them.

The versions of ourselves we leave behind.

The Things I Don’t Say Aloud is a collection of those moments.

If you have ever carried a feeling you could not quite explain, perhaps you will find a little of yourself here.

About

Hi, I’m Jennifer.

I’m a writer. But that is only one part of my life.

I’m also an entrepreneur, which means a lot of my days are spent dealing with very unromantic things — production, customers, numbers, problems, deadlines and figuring out what to do when something inevitably goes wrong.

And then I come home and write.

I sometimes think the two lives should contradict each other. They don’t.

Business taught me to observe people. Writing taught me to understand them a little better.

I have always been curious about why people do the things they do, why certain memories refuse to leave us, why some relationships change us completely, and why we can know something intellectually long before our hearts catch up.

That curiosity finds its way into almost everything I write.

I love poetry because it can say a great deal with very little.

I love fiction because I can create someone entirely imaginary and somehow discover something real through them.

And I love writing here because not every thought needs to become a book before it deserves to exist.

Outside all of that, I am someone who is constantly starting things.

Businesses. Ideas. Stories. Projects.

Some survive. Some don’t. A few become very important to me.

I love animals, long conversations, beautiful places, strange ideas, humour, creating things from nothing, and the feeling when something that existed only in your head finally becomes real.

This website is one of those things.

So if you have found your way here, welcome.

I hope you find something that makes you stop for a moment and think, “I’ve felt that too.”
