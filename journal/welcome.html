Welcome to my journal

29 September 2026 · Jennifer Navis

This is a new little corner of the internet for the things I want to write before I know exactly what they will become.

There will be poems here. Stories. Personal reflections. Pieces from works in progress. Some things may eventually find their way into books. Others may simply belong to the week in which they were written.

I wanted a place that felt less like an announcement and more like an open notebook — somewhere I could return to regularly, write honestly, and let the work grow in public.

So, welcome. Read what stays with you. Leave a thought if you feel like it. And come back when there are more words.
