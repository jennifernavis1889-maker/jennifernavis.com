Who Am I?

4 October 2026 · Jennifer Navis · 1 min read

I have been many people
Without ever knowing it.

Rude to one.
Polite to another.
A flirt to some.
Nonchalant to others.
Gallant to a few.

An innert, to someone
Who noticed how much of me
Remains unspoken.

But have you ever thought
That perhaps none of you is wrong,
Yet none of you is right?

You've known me in pieces
And mistaken them for the whole.

I thought I knew the whole.
After all, I was there.

I knew what I meant
When I said those words.
I knew why I stayed silent,
Why I walked away.

But in every story I tell myself,
I get to explain my part.

What happens to that version of me
When someone else tells the story?

Would I recognise myself?
Would I like who I saw?

And if you heard your own story
From the other side,
Would you?

And who am I to myself?
